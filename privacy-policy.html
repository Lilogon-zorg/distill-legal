Mumble Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · Effective November 2, 2026 · This document is a draft for review by counsel

Mumble is a voice-dictation app for Android. You speak; we return polished text in Hebrew, English and other languages. To do that we process your voice and the text made from it, so we want you to know exactly what happens to your data, who receives it and why, how long it is kept, and how to delete it. This document is written in plain language on purpose. If anything is unclear, write to us.

1. Who we are and how to reach us

The controller of your data (within the meaning of Israel's Protection of Privacy Law) is Or Weis, a registered sole proprietor in Israel trading as Lilogon ("we", "Mumble"). Postal address: 2 Hever St., Ramat Gan, Israel. Privacy contact: lilogon.dev@gmail.com. We answer privacy requests within 30 days.

2. What you should know before you start (notice under section 11 of Israel's Protection of Privacy Law)

  • Providing data is voluntary. No law requires you to give us information. Without a voice recording there is nothing to transcribe, and without signing in with a Google account you cannot use cloud dictation. The regular typing keyboard works without an account.
  • Purposes: transcribing your speech into text and polishing it; keeping your history and syncing it between your devices; managing your account, plan and payment; securing the service and preventing abuse; improving reliability and quality using technical metrics only.
  • Who receives the data and why: the recording goes to a transcription service (Soniox in the United States; if it fails, or for the instant preview, also Groq in the United States). The transcript goes to a language model that polishes it (Google Gemini API; if it fails, Groq). Your account, synced history and metrics are kept on our server hosted by Render. Details in sections 5 and 6.
  • Your rights: you may access the data we hold about you (section 13 of the Law) and ask us to correct or delete it (section 14). Details in section 10.
  • What we do not do: we do not train models on your voice or text, we do not identify people by their voice, we do not sell data, and we show no ads.

3. What happens when you dictate

  1. Recording. The app records your voice only while you tap (or hold) the microphone. The recording is first saved on your device, in the app's private storage, so it is not lost if the connection drops.
  2. Transcription. The recording is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and from there to the transcription service. Our server holds the audio in memory only and never writes it to disk.
  3. Polishing. The raw transcript, together with your settings (cleanup style, custom instructions, personal dictionary, translation target if requested), is sent to a language model that fixes punctuation, removes filler words and adapts the text. When you dictate from the keyboard inside another app, a short piece of the text already in the field around the cursor (up to 200 characters before and after) is sent along so that punctuation and capitalisation fit. That piece is not stored.
  4. Result. The raw and polished text return to your device and are shown or inserted into the field. If history is on, they are saved on the device and synced to your account.

"Voice-aware cleanup" (Voice-aware mode): if you turn this option on in Settings, the recording itself (not only the transcript) is also sent to Google's Gemini model. It is off by default.

No provider may use your voice to identify or verify anyone.

6. Transfers outside Israel

The providers in section 5 operate outside Israel, mostly in the United States, whose law is not recognised in Israel as providing equivalent protection. Transfers rest on two things: your consent, given on the onboarding screen before your first recording (and withdrawable at any time by stopping use and deleting your account), and written agreements with each provider under which it undertakes to use the data only to provide the service to us, keep it confidential, secure it, not pass it to others except sub-processors bound by the same undertakings, and enable us to honour your rights of access, correction and deletion. We check yearly that these undertakings remain in force.

7. Private mode, auto-private mode and the onboarding demo

  • Private mode: when on, the dictation is not saved to history or synced, and the recording is deleted from the device as soon as processing ends. The providers in section 5 still process the dictation to return your text, but nothing of it remains with us.
  • Auto-private mode: when on, Mumble switches to private mode by itself in fields that the host app marks as private or incognito. The same rules apply.
  • Onboarding demo: you can try Mumble before signing in. The demo recording is processed by the same providers under the same rules and is not linked to an account; neither the recording nor the text is stored. We keep only an anonymous technical metric (timings, provider, outcome).

8. Cloud sync and encryption

Your history, dictionary and settings are stored on our server so they are available on all your devices. In the database they are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a separate key per user derived from a master key kept outside the database. Please understand: this is server-side encryption, not end-to-end encryption. Our server can decrypt the data in order to deliver it to you, so we treat it as sensitive: access to the key is restricted, no person reads users' histories, and logs and metrics contain no content. On the device, history is kept in an encrypted database (SQLCipher) whose key is protected by the Android Keystore, and the audio vault lives in the app's private storage, which the operating system protects. Android backup of app data is disabled.

10. Your rights

  • Access: you may obtain a copy of your data. In the app: Settings → Account → Export my data (a JSON file with your history, dictionary, settings and account details). By email: within 30 days, in Hebrew, Arabic or English.
  • Correction and deletion: you can edit and delete any entry, dictionary word and setting directly in the app. Other requests by email are answered within 30 days. If we refuse a request we explain why in writing within 30 days and note your right to appeal to the Magistrates' Court.
  • Account deletion: section 11.
  • Withdrawing consent: you may stop using the service and delete your account at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already took place.
  • Complaints: you may contact Israel's Privacy Protection Authority (www.gov.il/en/departments/the_privacy_protection_authority). We would be glad to try to resolve the matter directly first.

11. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account → confirm. Without the app: at https://lilogon-zorg.github.io/distill-legal/delete-account or by email to lilogon.dev@gmail.com from the address you signed up with; we verify the request, delete within 30 days and confirm in writing.

Deleted immediately: the account (ID, email, name), synced history, dictionary, settings, usage counters and usage events; we also ask RevenueCat to delete the subscriber record. Technical metrics are unlinked from your identity. On the device, the encrypted database, the audio vault and the encryption keys are erased. Active sign-ins are revoked. What remains, and why: de-identified technical metrics (reliability), payment records at Google and with us as far as the law requires (for example tax law), and copies in backups that expire by themselves within 7 days. Deleting the account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription; cancel it before deleting (Terms, section 12) or through Google Play.

12. Security

All communication is encrypted (TLS; unencrypted traffic is blocked in the app). Server data is encrypted as described in section 8. Access to the systems is limited to the business owner and protected by multi-factor authentication. Provider access keys are kept on the server only and are not in the app. We log security events and review the configuration yearly. If a security incident that may affect you is discovered, we will notify you without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after we establish that your data is affected, and we will report it to the Privacy Protection Authority (also where a report is not mandatory).

13. Children

Mumble is intended for users aged 16 and over. If you are under 18, buying a subscription requires a parent's or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16; if we learn of such a registration we delete the account. Parents may contact us by email.

14. Users in the European Union, the EEA and the United Kingdom

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the GDPR (or UK GDPR) also applies to you. The controller is described in section 1. Legal bases: performance of our contract with you (dictation, sync, account, billing), our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, technical metrics), consent (marketing, if ever) and legal obligation (tax records). Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to complain to the supervisory authority in your country. Israel is recognised by the European Commission and by the United Kingdom as providing an adequate level of protection, so transfers to us are permitted; onward transfers to providers in the United States rest on those providers' data-processing agreements, which include the EU standard contractual clauses. We make no automated decisions with legal effects about you.

15. Messages and marketing

We send operational messages only: confirmations, trial-end notices, changes to these documents and security incidents. We will not send marketing email or notifications without your separate, explicit consent, and every such message will offer a simple way to opt out.

16. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy materially (for example a new provider, a new purpose or a longer retention period), we show a notice in the app before the change takes effect and ask for your consent again, and we update the effective date and the version history below. The Hebrew version is binding for users in Israel; for all other users the English version is binding.
