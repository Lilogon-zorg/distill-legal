Delete your Mumble account and data

Mumble · Lilogon · Updated: November 2, 2026

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Mumble → Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email lilogon.dev@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my account". We verify the request, delete the account within 30 days and confirm in writing.

What is deleted

The account (ID, email, name), synced history, dictionary, settings, usage counters, usage events and the subscriber record at our subscription-management provider. De-identified technical metrics remain for reliability. Copies in backups expire by themselves within 7 days. Payment records are kept at Google and with us as far as the law requires. If you delete through the app, the encrypted database and recordings on the device are erased too.

Please note

Deleting the account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or send us a cancellation notice (below). Deletion cannot be undone.

Cancel a subscription

Cancel any time in Google Play, or by notice to lilogon.dev@gmail.com with your name and the account's email (users in Israel: also your ID number, as the law requires). Cancellation by notice to us takes effect within 3 business days. Details in the Terms, section 6.
